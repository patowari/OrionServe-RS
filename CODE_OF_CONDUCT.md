# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep criticism directed at code
and ideas rather than at people. Technical disagreement is welcome and expected;
personal attacks, harassment, and demeaning behaviour are not.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers via GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that
violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
